How We Started

From The Court To The Closet

Sodo Dumaki started with a simple frustration: athletic wear that looked good on the court rarely looked good off it, and "designer" labels rarely held up through a real training session. We set out to build one brand that could do both — performance fabric engineering paired with the kind of detailing you'd expect from a runway, not a rack.

Every piece in the collection is designed around movement first, then finished with the color story that makes Sodo Dumaki instantly recognizable: deep royal purple, polished gold, and a black-and-grey foundation that never goes out of style.
