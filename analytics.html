Read Before You Rely On This

This dashboard only reflects orders and bulk requests submitted in this browser — there's no shared database yet, so it will not show your real, full customer activity across all devices/browsers. For real analytics once the site is live:

  • Square Dashboard → Reports already breaks down sales by customer and location once payments run through Square.
  • Google Analytics (or Meta Pixel) shows where your site traffic comes from geographically, including visitors who don't buy.
  • A real backend + database is what would let this exact dashboard show truthful, cross-device numbers — see README.md.
