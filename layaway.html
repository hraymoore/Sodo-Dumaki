Good To Know

Business Layaway FAQ

Who qualifies?

Layaway is available for business/bulk uniform orders meeting our 100-piece minimum — teams, schools, and organizations placing a custom order through the Business page.

When does my order start?

Production begins the moment your 60% deposit clears — not before. Nothing ships until it's paid in full.

When is the balance due?

The remaining 40% is invoiced by email as soon as your order is complete and ready to ship.

Can I cancel?

Yes, before production begins. Once your deposit is applied and production has started, deposits are non-refundable.

Is there interest?

Never. Layaway is a payment schedule, not a loan — no interest, no credit check, no fees.

What about individual retail orders?

Retail orders from the shop pay in full at checkout via Square. Layaway is reserved for qualifying business and bulk uniform orders.
